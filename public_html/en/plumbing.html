A CLEARLY DEFINED SCOPE

From building requirements to installation.

Plumbing systems need to support everyday building use and remain accessible for checks and interventions. We prepare cold water, hot water and wastewater installation by aligning routes with the architecture, technical rooms and equipment positions defined in the design.

For housing, offices and industrial spaces, we discuss consumption points, vertical and horizontal routes and areas that will be enclosed by finishes. Materials, connections and testing stages are agreed before installation. Industrial process requirements or specialist equipment are reviewed separately against their documentation.

What we address at handover

  • Connections and work boundaries clarified
  • Agreed tests before relevant routes are enclosed
  • Contracted handover documents and observations

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for a clear start.

What can plumbing work include?

It can cover cold and hot water distribution, internal drainage and sanitary fixture connections, within the agreed design and accepted proposal.

When are tests carried out?

Testing stages are agreed with the project team before the relevant routes are concealed. The technical solution and project requirements determine the checks.

Do you work on renovations and fit-outs?

Yes, following review of available networks and documentation. Connections to existing installations and any shutdowns are agreed before work starts.
