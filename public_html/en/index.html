FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for a clear start.

What does VASTASIA do?

Electrical, plumbing, heating, ventilation and air conditioning installations, together with installation planning and technical coordination between disciplines.

Where does VASTASIA work?

The company is based in Bucharest and has project experience in Romania, Germany and Austria. Availability and arrangements for a new site are discussed at the first meeting.

How can I request a proposal?

Call +40 726 425 892 or email office@vastasia.ro. The location, building type, project stage and available drawings are useful starting points.

Do you have international project experience?

Yes. The portfolio includes office spaces in Bremen and Nuremberg, an industrial project in Vienna and a residential project in Voluntari, Ilfov.
