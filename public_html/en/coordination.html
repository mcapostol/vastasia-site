A CLEARLY DEFINED SCOPE

From building requirements to installation.

Technical coordination connects client requirements, project documentation and site execution. Electrical, plumbing and HVAC teams often need the same areas. Clarifying interfaces and the sequence of interventions helps prepare coherent installation work.

We review available documents, identify decisions and organise responsibilities by stage. Where design or document adaptation is needed, we define a separate phase and the specialists involved. The proposal sets the scope and deliverables in line with the project’s maturity.

What we address at handover

  • Agreed clarification and interface list
  • Work sequence and assigned responsibilities
  • Compilation of agreed installation and handover documents

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for a clear start.

Does coordination replace design?

No. Coordination organises interfaces and installation. Design, design checking and other regulated roles are defined separately for the project.

Can you review a project already under way?

We can review its progress, available documents and installed work. Before a proposal, we establish what can be taken over and what needs clarification or checking.

How does collaboration start?

Contact us by phone or email and describe your objective. We discuss the context, documents and schedule, then define the scope and proposal by discipline.
